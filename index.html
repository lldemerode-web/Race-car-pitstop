♜ Chrono Chess

Multiverse chess · playable engine prototype

How to play this prototype

Select a piece on a board marked PLAYABLE, then select a highlighted square on that board or another board. Time travel to a historical board creates a new timeline. Each board is a snapshot, so moving creates successor boards rather than overwriting history. Submit your turn after moving on the earliest active boards. This prototype does not yet implement the complete official rules (check/checkmate, castling, en passant, promotions, all pawn temporal rules, or the exact present/active timeline submission algorithm).
